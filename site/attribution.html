Reuse and independence

The catalogue provides links and attribution, without endorsement by any source organization. Upstream license labels apply to the upstream materials, not automatically to every external publication or to the entire catalogue.

Before copying upstream material, follow its applicable license: retain notices and supply the license for Apache-2.0 material; provide credit, a license link, and modification notices for CC BY material. Third-party material needs its own terms checked. Linking here does not grant a new license to a paper.

The shared Apache-2.0 license text is included for reference. Local proof audits and independent mathematical statement review have not been performed on these external artifacts.
