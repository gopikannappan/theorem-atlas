# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and mathematical authors with respect. Keep criticism focused on claims, evidence, and code. Be clear about uncertainty and distinguish a mathematical disagreement from a personal judgment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Do not publish private correspondence, credentials, or identifying information without permission. Credit prior work and avoid unsupported accusations of misconduct.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or block accounts to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate, and people affected should have an opportunity to explain or appeal when practical. This policy covers repository issues, PRs, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the current maintainer through GitHub's available reporting/contact channels; do not expose the affected person's information in a public issue. GitHub's abuse reporting is available when direct contact is not appropriate.
